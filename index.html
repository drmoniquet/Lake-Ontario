The poems, made up only of the letters in Lake Ontario, and inspired by the US' renaming of this shared body of water on August 27, 2026, are set inside an outline of the lake. In the upper right is Prince Edward County; the gap along the lower edge represents the New York shore, which at that latitude divides the water. The poems' constraints mean that they cannot say the, or and, or water, and that nothing in them is plural, past, or ongoing. Which seems to be an apt allegory of our political situation.

— Monique Tschofen
